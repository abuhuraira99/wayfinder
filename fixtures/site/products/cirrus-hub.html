Cirrus Hub

The Cirrus Hub collects readings from nearby sensors and forwards them to Halcyon Cloud. A single hub manages up to 64 sensors.

Specifications

  • Backhaul: Ethernet and LTE-M
  • Power: PoE (802.3af) or 12 V DC
  • Radio: LoRaWAN, EU868 and US915
  • Unit price: $599
